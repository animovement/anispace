# Turn every subject to face the way its declared orientation says

Turn every subject to face the way its declared orientation says

## Usage

``` r
rotate_onto_orientation(data, member, level, align_perpendicular)
```

## Arguments

- data:

  An aniframe, already centred on `member`.

- member:

  The member whose orientation gives the subject's.

- level:

  The identity variable it belongs to.

- align_perpendicular:

  Face across `+x` rather than along it.

## Value

`data`, rotated.
